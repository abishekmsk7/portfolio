View from Seat

A feature I built on top of Tessitura seat selection: before booking, customers can see the sightline from the seat they’ve picked. This simplified version is a Vue component, and the view redraws from the seat’s row and position.

  • Built on top of Tessitura TNEW seat selection, as part of the venue’s booking journey
  • Helps customers choose with confidence, especially for seats with restricted or angled views
